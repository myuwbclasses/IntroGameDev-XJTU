Final submission

You must submit something like this. Note:

  • Images and files can be on any online server. They do not need to be on Google Drive.
  • Each game is highlighted with an embedded (iframe) video. Please include a short 60–90 second video; it can be hosted on Bilibili.
  • Here is a guide to creating and hosting sites (e.g., WebGL games) on GitHub.
  • Remember to include your Chinese names!
